# Rentamigo Bangalore Residential Property & Lease Management SOP

## 1. Standard Lease & Security Deposit Clauses
- **Standard Lock-In Period:** Residential leases carry a mandatory **6-month lock-in period** from the agreement start date. Vacating during the lock-in period forfeits the security deposit unless mutually waived in writing by the owner.
- **Notice Period:** Post lock-in, either party must serve a **2-month (60-day) written notice** via email or registered portal ticket. Shortfall in notice period is adjusted pro-rata against the security deposit.
- **Security Deposit Refund SLA:** Security deposits are refunded within **15 working days** post key-handover and move-out inspection, after deducting unpaid utility bills and documented damages beyond normal wear and tear.
- **Annual Rent Escalation:** Standard renewal escalation is capped at **5% to 7%** every 11 months.

## 2. Maintenance & SLA Triage Matrix
- **P0 — Critical Emergency (SLA: 2 Hours):** Active water pipe burst, electrical short-circuit/sparking, main door lock failure, or gas leak. Action: Immediate vendor dispatch + notify Property Manager on call.
- **P1 — Urgent Functional Issue (SLA: 24 Hours):** Geyser malfunction, refrigerator/washing machine breakdown (furnished units), bathroom drainage clog, or inverter battery failure. Action: Schedule technician within 24 hours.
- **P2 — Routine / Cosmetic (SLA: 72 Hours):** Cabinet hinge alignment, minor wall paint touch-up, balcony mesh repair, or routine AC servicing. Action: Log in weekly maintenance batch.
- **Tenant-Borne Consumables:** Replacement of fused LED bulbs, RO water filter candles (after 6 months), and pest control due to hygiene lapses are borne by the tenant.

## 3. Escalation & Legal Guardrails
- **Human Escalation Triggers (`escalate_to_human: true`):**
  1. Any dispute involving security deposit deductions exceeding **₹10,000**.
  2. Requests for early lock-in waiver, rent reduction, or fee waivers.
  3. Threats of legal notice, police complaint, or RERA/consumer court escalation.
  4. Any question not explicitly covered in the uploaded lease text or this SOP (e.g., Airbnb subletting, commercial usage).
- **Strict Prohibition:** Never authorize fee waivers, alter lock-in clauses, or promise exact refund figures before physical move-out inspection.
